About OuterGuide Travel

Our History

Founded in 2015, OuterGuide Travel was born out of a desire to make multi-continent travel less complicated. We started by designing complex Euro-Asian rail and flight routes and have since expanded to cover the entire globe.

Our Mission

To connect people across oceans and borders by providing expertly curated, stress-free intercontinental travel packages.

Our Vision

A globally connected world where navigating between continents is as seamless as traveling to the next town.
